# End-to-End Game Generation: Methods and Benchmarks

[Home](../../README.md) · [中文](../zh-CN/end-to-end.md) · [Automated design & PCG](pcg.md) · [Interactive worlds](interactive-worlds.md) · [Scope](../../SCOPE.md)

> Research cutoff: **2026-09-04 (Asia/Shanghai)**. This page contains **34 canonical records**: eight formal generation benchmarks and 26 methods whose central contribution directly produces a runnable game, game project or code, or executable rules and mechanics.

Existing-repository coding benchmarks, framework-only proposals, verifier/repair/balancing-only work, non-playable scenes, asset-only work, and game-playing research are excluded. **Open**, **Partial**, and **Closed** describe verified official artifact availability, not paper quality.

## Explicit exclusions

- Benchmarks for agents that play existing games, and NPC/player-policy papers.
- Standalone verifiers, repair/QA systems, datasets, corpora, surveys, taxonomies, position papers, and infrastructure.
- Asset/component generation unless the paper's method integrates it into a runnable game.
- Learned interactive worlds, which have their own generation-method page.
