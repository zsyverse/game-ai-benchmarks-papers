# Automated Game Design and PCG: Methods and Benchmarks

[Home](../../README.md) · [中文](../zh-CN/pcg.md) · [End-to-end generation](end-to-end.md) · [Interactive worlds](interactive-worlds.md) · [Scope](../../SCOPE.md) · [Compact list](paper-list.md#automated-design-and-pcg) · [Research guide](research-guide.md) · [Sources](../../research/pcg-automated-design-sources.md)

> This page contains **120 canonical records**: 18 complete-game/rule/mechanic generation methods and 102 level/playable-content methods or formal generation benchmarks.

A paper is included only when its central contribution directly generates executable rules, complete games, or playable levels/maps/tasks/charts, or formally benchmarks one of those tasks. Dataset-only, survey, metric-only, infrastructure, repair/tuning-only, reskin, incomplete-component, and narrative-only work is excluded.
