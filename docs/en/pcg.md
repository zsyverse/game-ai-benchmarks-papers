# Procedural Game Generation and Automated Game Design

[Home](../../README.md) · [中文](../zh-CN/pcg.md) · [End-to-end generation](end-to-end.md) · [Interactive worlds](interactive-worlds.md) · [Scope](../../SCOPE.md)

> Research cutoff: **2026-08-29 (Asia/Shanghai)**. This index is limited to work that generates games, executable rules or mechanics, levels, or game-world components. It excludes papers whose main task is only to play an existing game.

Automated play, self-play, search, and learning agents appear only when they **evaluate generated designs**. Reinforcement learning appears only when the policy **generates or edits content**, as in PCGRL; playing-only RL benchmarks and agent leaderboards are out of scope. **Open** means that the central official artifacts are public; **Partial** means that useful artifacts exist but the complete historical or experimental pipeline is missing; **Paper-only** means that no official runnable release was verified.

## Explicit exclusions

- Playing-only benchmarks and agent-control tasks—including ALE, Procgen, CoinRun, NetHack Learning Environment, MineRL/BASALT, Crafter, BabyAI, and GVGAI playing tracks—are excluded.
- MCTS, RL, search, or self-play papers are not included merely because they play or evaluate a fixed game. They appear above only when they evaluate a generated design or directly generate/optimize content.
- Standalone textures, sprites, music, dialogue, stories, videos, and next-frame world models are not complete-game generation; at most they belong to component generation.
