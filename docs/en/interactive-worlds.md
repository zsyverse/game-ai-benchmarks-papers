# Interactive Game World Generation: Papers and Resources

[Home](../../README.md) · [中文](../zh-CN/interactive-worlds.md) · [End-to-end generation](end-to-end.md) · [Automated design & PCG](pcg.md) · [Scope](../../SCOPE.md)

> Research cutoff: **2026-08-29 (Asia/Shanghai)**. This index defines the **learned/pixel world-generation boundary**: systems generate an action-conditioned game world's observations, video, geometry, or explicit state. It is deliberately separate from **code/rules game generation**, which produces source code, engine projects, levels, or executable rule descriptions, and from game-playing research whose main result is a policy or score.

Both prompt-to-new-world systems and learned simulators of existing games are included, but the distinction is stated in each entry. **Open** means official training and inference code, runnable weights, and meaningful data/evaluation artifacts are available; **Partial** means a material component is missing; **Closed** means no runnable official checkpoint and core implementation were verified.

## 1. Foundational generative engines (8)

| Year | Paper/resource | Generation scope/action conditioning | Evaluation | Artifacts/status |
| :---: | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2024 | [Genie — Generative Interactive Environments](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/bruce24a.html) | Learns eight latent actions from unlabelled platformer video and rolls out a playable 2D world from one image, sketch, or photograph; generation is conditioned on the prompt frame, prior tokens, and a user-selected latent action. | FVD, action-effect `ΔPSNR`, scaling studies, and out-of-distribution prompt tests. | **Closed** — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.15391), [project](https://sites.google.com/view/genie-2024/home); no dataset, weights, or implementation. |
| 2024 | [GameNGen — Diffusion Models Are Real-Time Game Engines](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.14837) | An action-conditioned diffusion model replaces Doom's renderer and transition loop, generating frames from 64 prior frames and discrete action history at about 20 FPS; it simulates an existing game rather than authoring new rules. | Teacher-forced and autoregressive PSNR/LPIPS, 16/32-frame FVD, speed, and a blinded human real-versus-simulation study. | **Closed** — [project](https://gamengen.github.io/), [project-page source](https://github.com/GameNGen/GameNGen.github.io); presentation assets only, without model, data, code, or weights. |
| 2024 | [Oasis — A Universe in a Transformer](https://oasis-model.github.io/) | Generates a Minecraft-like world autoregressively from an initial frame and discrete keyboard controls; the released 500M model is smaller than the hosted system. | Live interaction and qualitative rollouts; no fixed test suite or reproducible leaderboard. | **Partial** — [inference code](https://github.com/etched-ai/open-oasis), [500M weights](https://huggingface.co/Etched/oasis-500m); strongest model, training stack/data, and evaluator are unavailable. |
| 2025 | [GameGen-X — Interactive Open-world Game Video Generation](https://openreview.net/forum?id=8VG8tpPZhe) | Supports open-domain text-to-game-video generation and context-video continuation conditioned on structured instructions/events, rather than a universal low-level keyboard schema. | FID/FVD, text-video alignment, quality/dynamics/consistency metrics, OGameEval-Gen/OGameEval-Ins, user preference, and control success. | **Partial** — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00769), [project](https://gamegen-x.github.io/), [repository and OGameData metadata](https://github.com/GameGen-X/GameGen-X); model code/weights and the internal instruction subset are unavailable. |
| 2025 | [GameFactory — Creating New Games with Generative Interactive Videos](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.08325) | Transfers Minecraft-derived W/S/A/D, jump/sneak/sprint, and continuous mouse controls into visually new first-person scenes using an open-domain video prior. | Camera-pose and optical-flow error, CLIP similarity, FID/FVD, domain score, rare-action combinations, and autoregressive rollouts. | **Partial** — [project](https://yujiwen.github.io/gamefactory/), [repository](https://github.com/KlingAIResearch/GameFactory), [GF-Minecraft dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/KwaiVGI/GameFactory-Dataset); data/utilities are public, but model code and weights are not. |
| 2025 | [MineWorld — a Real-Time and Open-Source Interactive World Model on Minecraft](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.08388) | Interleaves visual and 11-way movement/camera/control tokens, using parallel diagonal decoding to generate Minecraft observations at 4–7 FPS. | FVD/visual metrics, inverse-dynamics action classification, camera-movement error, and decoding throughput. | **Partial** — [code and evaluation](https://github.com/microsoft/mineworld), [model location](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/mineworld); inference/metrics remain public, but checkpoints were removed and training code/data are absent. |
| 2025–2026 | [Matrix-Game 1.0](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.18701) / [2.0](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.13009) / [3.0](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08995) | Evolves from Minecraft generation to multi-game real-time streaming and 720p long-horizon memory, conditioned on keyboard states plus continuous mouse/camera motion. | GameWorld Score, human preference, revisitation/memory tests, VAE PSNR/SSIM, and throughput up to about 40 FPS in the 3.0 setup. | **Partial** — [code](https://github.com/SkyworkAI/Matrix-Game), [1.0 models](https://huggingface.co/Skywork/Matrix-Game), [2.0 models](https://huggingface.co/Skywork/Matrix-Game-2.0), [3.0 models](https://huggingface.co/Skywork/Matrix-Game-3.0), [1.0 project](https://matrix-game-homepage.github.io/), [2.0 project](https://matrix-game-v2.github.io/), [3.0 project](https://matrix-game-v3.github.io/); inference and selected weights are public, but full training/data and strongest 3.0 models are not. |
| 2025–2026 | [Hunyuan-GameCraft 1.0](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.17201) / [GameCraft-2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.23429) | Generates high-dynamic multi-game video from a reference image, text, and keyboard/mouse/camera signals; 2.0 adds free-form, multi-turn instructions such as opening doors or triggering explosions. | FVD, VBench-style quality/dynamics/consistency, camera RPE, speed and human study; 2.0 adds InterBench instruction, fluency, end-state, and physics scores. | **Partial (1.0) / Closed (2.0)** — [1.0 project](https://hunyuan-gamecraft.github.io/), [1.0 code](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan-GameCraft-1.0), [1.0 weights](https://huggingface.co/tencent/Hunyuan-GameCraft-1.0), [2.0 project](https://hunyuan-gamecraft-2.github.io/), [2.0 hosted demo](https://hunyuan.tencent.com/game/game-craft); 1.0 lacks full training/data, while 2.0 has no downloadable code or checkpoint. |

## 2. Long-horizon, multiplayer, mechanics, and NPC generation (8)

| Year | Paper/resource | Generation scope/action conditioning | Evaluation | Artifacts/status |
| :---: | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2026 | [Solaris — Building a Multiplayer Video World Model in Minecraft](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.22208) | Generates two synchronized Minecraft first-person streams from paired initial observations and each player's controls, exposing shared-world movement, building, memory, and cross-view consistency. | Held-out Movement, Grounding, Memory, Building, and Consistency episodes, FID, and a released VLM self-consistency metric. | **Open** — [project](https://solaris-wm.github.io/), [training/inference/evaluation](https://github.com/solaris-wm/solaris), [collection engine](https://github.com/solaris-wm/solaris-engine), [models and data](https://huggingface.co/collections/nyu-visionx/solaris-models); full pretraining also depends on separately distributed VPT data. |
| 2026 | [WorldCam — Interactive Autoregressive 3D Gaming Worlds](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16871) | Uses a shared 6-DoF camera-pose representation for physics-based keyboard/mouse control and retrieval of past views, reducing geometric drift during long-term revisitation. | Translation/rotation RPE, VBench++, PSNR/LPIPS/MEt3R/DINO, sharpness, 200-frame tests, speed, and a blinded user study. | **Partial** — [project](https://cvlab-kaist.github.io/WorldCam/), [inference](https://github.com/cvlab-kaist/WorldCam), [weights](https://huggingface.co/worldcam/worldcam), [open-game recordings](https://huggingface.co/datasets/worldcam/worldcam-dataset); training code and the paper's training data are unavailable. |
| 2026 | [ReactiveGWM — Steering NPC in Reactive Game World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15256) | Separately conditions Street Fighter rollouts on low-level player actions and prompt-level NPC strategies, enabling steerable opponent reactions and cross-game transfer. | Player Move/Attack accuracy, Gemini/Qwen instruction accuracy, SSIM/LPIPS, zero-shot transfer, and a 19-person study. | **Open** — [project](https://inv-wzq.github.io/ReactiveGWM/), [training/inference](https://github.com/INV-WZQ/ReactiveGWM), [models](https://huggingface.co/INV-WZQ/ReactiveGWM-Models), [datasets](https://huggingface.co/datasets/INV-WZQ/ReactiveGWM-Datasets). |
| 2026 | [SCOPE — Simulating Cross-game Operations in Playable Environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23345) | Generates 480×832 FPS clips across seven games from an image, prompt, and frame-aligned 10-DoF gamepad telemetry, separating local weapon effects from global movement/camera motion. | CrossFPS's 1,378 clips, dynamics/flow, smoothness/depth, JEPA/FVD/LPIPS, action composition, scaling, and zero-shot scenes. | **Partial** — [project](https://z2tong.github.io/SCOPE/), [inference](https://github.com/z2tong/SCOPE), [weights](https://huggingface.co/zizhaotong/SCOPE), [CrossFPS](https://huggingface.co/collections/zizhaotong/crossfps); no training code. |
| 2026 | [StatePlay — State-Aware Game World Models for Mechanics-Consistent Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26754) | Jointly predicts Street Fighter video and five synchronized variables such as health, timer, and skill meters, conditioning visuals on player actions and predicted state. | SSIM/LPIPS, Move/Attack accuracy, normalized state distance/alignment, and Gemini/GPT mechanics-fidelity judgments. | **Open** — [project](https://jimntu.github.io/stateplay_page/), [training/inference](https://github.com/Jimntu/StatePlay), [model](https://huggingface.co/onepiece1999/StatePlay), [dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/onepiece1999/StatePlay-Dataset); VLM judging still depends on proprietary model versions. |
| 2026 | [ForgeWM — Progressive Causal Training for Few-Step Action-Conditioned Video World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14022) | Releases a four-stage recipe for 1-, 2-, and 4-step real-time Minecraft/FPS models conditioned on images and keyboard/mouse streams, plus replay-time trajectory refinement. | VBench, LPIPS/flow, keyboard/mouse accuracy, latency/FPS, replay fidelity, a 41-person study, and seven-game CrossFPS transfer. | **Open** — [project](https://asdfo123.github.io/ForgeWM/), [full code](https://github.com/asdfo123/ForgeWM), [models](https://huggingface.co/ForgeWM/ForgeWM), [prepared data](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ForgeWM/ForgeWM-data); upstream licenses still apply. |
| 2026 | [WorldMind — Decoupled Game World Model for State-Aware NPC Behavior](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21439) | Closes the loop between state reconstruction, NPC planning, action translation, and about-20-FPS boss-fight video so NPC behavior reacts to evolving generated state. | State reconstruction, decision sensitivity/validity, pairwise preference, action validity, and tactical sequence fit. | **Closed** — [project](https://teawhite.cn/WorldMind/), [release stub](https://github.com/TeaWhiteBro/WorldMind); code, weights, BOSS-140K, and evaluator are not downloadable. |
| 2026 | [Marionette — Predicting World States, Rendering Geometry, Painting Appearance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14530) | Predicts a 276-D articulated multi-character state from action IDs, deterministically renders geometry, and uses diffusion only to paint long-horizon RGB observations. | Joint error, character separation, ground penetration, rule-based state repair, and observation FVD. | **Partial** — [project](https://alayalab.github.io/Marionette/), [inference/runtime](https://github.com/AlayaLab/Marionette), [weights](https://huggingface.co/AlayaLab/Marionette), [WildWorld source corpus](https://github.com/AlayaLab/WildWorld); training code and the full derived corpus are unavailable. |

## 3. Generation-enabling datasets and field framing (2)

| Year | Paper/resource | Generation scope/action conditioning | Evaluation | Artifacts/status |

| 2026 | [PhysEditWorld — A Large-Scale Dataset Toward Physics-Editable World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26694) | Replays identical UE5 initial states and actions under edited gravity, releasing paired RGB/depth/normals/audio/action/camera/state supervision for physics- and action-conditioned generation. | Proposed gravity-conditioned video, action-conditioned rollout, and gravity-aware video-language tasks; no fixed released evaluator yet. | **Partial** — [project](https://yizhiqianbi.github.io/physeditworld/), [release repository](https://github.com/yizhiqianbi/physeditworld), [ModelScope dataset](https://www.modelscope.cn/datasets/GelerCAT/PhysicalWorld); demo subsets/schema are public, while the full dataset, UE5 pipeline, and scripts remain planned. |

## Explicit playing/RL exclusions

The following are deliberately outside this generation-only index even when their learned models predict game observations:

- **DIAMOND — Diffusion for World Modeling: Visual Details Matter in Atari** ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.12399), [code](https://github.com/eloialonso/diamond)) centers on training and evaluating an RL agent by Atari-100k return.
- **Dreamer / DreamerV2 / DreamerV3, IRIS, and SimPLe** use predictive models primarily to improve policy learning, sample efficiency, or control return; the evaluated product is the player policy.
- **GameWAM — A World Action Model for Video Games** ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26200)) predicts observations and executable controls, but its core contribution is a closed-loop gameplay/GUI policy evaluated by task success.
- **ActSWM and similar planning models** are excluded when action selection or planning success, rather than a human-controllable generated environment, is the main result.
