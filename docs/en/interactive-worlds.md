# Interactive Game World Generation: Methods and Benchmarks

[Home](../../README.md) · [中文](../zh-CN/interactive-worlds.md) · [End-to-end generation](end-to-end.md) · [Automated design & PCG](pcg.md) · [Scope](../../SCOPE.md) · [Compact list](paper-list.md#interactive-game-worlds) · [Research guide](research-guide.md) · [Sources](../../research/interactive-world-generation-sources.md)

> This page contains **45 canonical records**: 41 methods that generate a player-controllable game world and four formal benchmarks that evaluate generated worlds.

The generated world must change step by step with player input. Dataset-only, renderer-only, prompted-video, survey/position, preprocessing, NPC/planning, and game-playing work is excluded. **Open**, **Partial**, and **Closed** describe verified official artifact availability.

## Explicit exclusions

- World models whose main result is policy learning, planning, NPC behavior, or game-playing success.
- Prompted game-video generators without a stepwise player-control loop.
- Datasets, renderers, HUD-removal/preprocessing systems, surveys, position papers, and metric-only work.
