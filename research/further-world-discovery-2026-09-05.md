Following review, four accepted world-method entries were integrated into the bilingual public index and canonical source dossier; unresolved candidates remain only in this note.

## Outcome and scope

**Accept four previously uncounted world-method records: Yume (2025), SANA-WM (2026), Wonder (2026), and Unbounded (2024).** These decisions follow newly located primary-source evidence, not a relaxed definition of a game. Keep Yume-1.5, RELIC, and minWM uncounted pending the specific evidence gaps below. Video2Game was independently reviewed on 2026-09-08 and integrated once as PCG #119 reconstructed playable content, not counted as a neural world-model record.

The test is the repository's [SCOPE](../SCOPE.md): a substantive generator, an input-conditioned continuation loop, explicit game-world output evidence, and evaluation of that generator. Navigation alone can qualify; combat, victory conditions, or a full conventional engine are not required. Conversely, merely training on Unreal Engine scenes, comparing with a game model, or naming an application “gaming” does not establish the generated output. A fixed one-shot camera-conditioned clip is insufficient without sequential generation evidence.

All six candidates were absent from the 41-record public interactive-world index at the start of this pass. Yume-1.5 is not automatically merged with Yume: its separate paper, new TSCM method, and direct comparison against Yume establish a distinct follow-up identity. SANA-WM's benchmark and method remain one paper record, as do Wonder's I2V/V2V benchmarks and its generator.

## 1. Yume: An Interactive World Generation Model

- **Year / venue / status:** 2025 arXiv preprint, [2507.17744](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.17744); inspected [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.17744), v1.
- **EN:** Generates explorable worlds from one image using quantized keyboard/camera actions, masked autoregressive diffusion, compressed memory, and improved sampling; the paper explicitly demonstrates generalization to video-game imagery.
- **中文：** 用量化键盘/相机动作、掩码自回归扩散、压缩记忆与改进采样，从单张图像生成可探索世界；原文明确展示向电子游戏图像的泛化。
- **Generates / action conditioning:** Fig. 1 says “continuous keyboard inputs”; §4.2 quantizes translation and rotation, while §5.2 uses generated history for continued video generation. §5.5.1 **World Generalization** explicitly states: “Though Yume is trained by real-world videos, it shows impressive generalizability to diverse unreal scenes, such as animation, video games, and AI-generated images.” This is an actual application subsection, not merely a related-work citation or intended future use. Scope is generated game-world exploration, not authored game rules.
- **Evaluation:** §6.1.2 builds Yume-Bench from 70 held-out Sekai-Real-HQ videos/images, including images for rare backward/tilt actions. Table 2 compares instruction following and VBench-like visual metrics against Wan-2.1 and MatrixGame; reported IF is 0.657. §6.2.2 studies 18-second rollouts: the initial eight seconds follow benchmark controls and the subsequent ten seconds move forward. Sampler/distillation ablations are in §6.3. The quantitative benchmark is predominantly real-world; do not present it as 70 game-specific tasks. The game-domain evidence is qualitative/generalization evidence in §5.5.1 and the visualization section.
- **Official artifacts:** [repository](https://github.com/stdstu12/YUME), [exact README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/stdstu12/YUME/main/README.md), [original 14B/540p model](https://huggingface.co/stdstu123/Yume-I2V-540P), [training entry](https://github.com/stdstu12/YUME/blob/main/scripts/finetune/finetune.sh), [SDE inference entry](https://github.com/stdstu12/YUME/blob/main/scripts/inference/sample_tts.sh). README documents MVDT/adversarial training, action preprocessing, source Sekai acquisition and inference. Its processed-dataset checkbox remains incomplete. The shared project page now foregrounds Yume-1.5; do not attribute the 5B model or 1.5's 12-FPS result to this paper.
- **Artifact payload check:** [HF API](https://huggingface.co/api/models/stdstu123/Yume-I2V-540P) returned `gated: false`, 24 files, seven `Yume-Dit/diffusion_pytorch_model-0000N-of-00007.safetensors` shards, VAE and text/vision encoders. Revision `4eb626b3f5c92e65f8b173c571b760a9ca7b1020`. These are weight-bearing artifacts, not an empty model card. No model binary was run or fully downloaded.
- **Openness:** **Partial.** Meaningful training/inference and original weights are public, but a complete processed dataset and paper-matched evaluation package were not verified. The paper's “fully open-source” wording is not substituted for this audit's artifact verdict.

## 2. SANA-WM: Efficient Minute-Scale World Modeling with Hybrid Linear Diffusion Transformer

- **Year / venue / status:** 2026 arXiv preprint, [2605.15178](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15178); inspected [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.15178), v1 dated May 14, 2026, plus the current official release.
- **EN:** Generates minute-scale camera-controlled worlds, including game-style environments, using a hybrid Gated DeltaNet/softmax backbone and dual-branch 6-DoF conditioning; distinct chunk-causal and distilled variants support sequential rollout.
- **中文：** 用 Gated DeltaNet/softmax 混合骨干与双分支 6-DoF 条件生成分钟级可控世界，包含游戏风格环境；独立的分块因果与蒸馏变体支持逐步续生成。
- **Generates / action conditioning:** §1 explicitly distinguishes an offline bidirectional generator, a chunk-causal autoregressive generator, and a few-step distilled autoregressive generator. §3.1 Stage 4 specifies chunk-causal finetuning, self-forcing, attention sinks and local windows; §3.2 prevents future-context leakage across chunk boundaries. Thus admission is based on the sequential variants, not the offline one-minute renderer alone. The current [official WM documentation](https://nvlabs.github.io/Sana/docs/sana_wm/) further describes chunk-causal DiT, chunk-causal refiner and causal-VAE decoding with progressively emitted chunks. This is camera-conditioned navigable generation, not native game-action semantics or complete game-rule generation.
- **Game-domain evidence:** §5.2 and Appendix D.1 explicitly define 80 initial images: 20 **game-style**, 20 indoor, 20 outdoor-city, and 20 outdoor-nature. Each has Simple/Hard revisit trajectories. This directly resolves the previous missing-game-output hold. The benchmark's generated game-style images need not be screenshots of named commercial games.
- **Evaluation:** Main 60-second benchmark has 80 scenes in each Simple/Hard split, at 16 FPS, with 960 video frames and 961 camera annotations per SANA-WM trajectory. Table 2 reports rotation/translation/CMC control, eight VBench dimensions and overall quality; Appendix E.1 evaluates PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS same-pose revisits and first-versus-last-ten-second quality degradation. Table 9 **separately compares bidirectional and autoregressive Stage-1** on the 60-second benchmark. Do not transfer the base/refined offline quality scores to the fastest distilled setting without its own result.
- **Runtime caveat:** The abstract's single-RTX-5090 NVFP4 result is **34 seconds to denoise a 60-second, 720p clip**, not a measured keyboard-to-first-frame latency. Main Table 2 throughput is reported under an eight-H100 aggregate evaluation budget, even when individual clips fit one GPU. Current docs distinguish a chunk-causal **teacher** whose default VAE/refiner path is still bidirectional from the separate fully streaming release. These configurations must not be conflated.
- **Official artifacts:** [repository](https://github.com/NVlabs/Sana), [WM documentation](https://nvlabs.github.io/Sana/docs/sana_wm/), [streaming weights](https://huggingface.co/Efficient-Large-Model/SANA-WM_streaming), [bidirectional weights](https://huggingface.co/Efficient-Large-Model/SANA-WM_bidirectional), [example training data](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Efficient-Large-Model/SANA-WM-example-training-dataset). The root README's July 2026 announcement explicitly releases **Stage-1** bidirectional/chunk-causal/distillation training. These are SANA-WM artifacts, not unrelated SANA image or ordinary video model weights.
- **Artifact payload check:** [streaming HF API](https://huggingface.co/api/models/Efficient-Large-Model/SANA-WM_streaming) returned `gated: false`, 25 files, including `sana_dit/model.pt`, `ltx2_causal_vae/diffusion_pytorch_model.safetensors`, refiner transformer/connectors and Gemma shards; revision `c7694938c854b1fcc29468e9514f1ea2d0c4ba8e`. [Bidirectional API](https://huggingface.co/api/models/Efficient-Large-Model/SANA-WM_bidirectional) returned 33 files and revision `e96271d77398def8ebb9fc595e7c0056dc625ab7`, including DiT/refiner/VAE weights. Model binaries were not executed.
- **Implementation check:** The actual [streaming inference source](https://github.com/NVlabs/Sana/blob/main/inference_video_scripts/wm/inference_sana_wm_streaming.py) was fetched (33,296 characters). Its header confirms chunk-causal student/refiner and causal-VAE blocks, using separate CUDA streams and progressive MP4 output. Its CLI accepts `--camera` pose arrays; this source check establishes causal generation but does not independently demonstrate a live keyboard front end. Do not claim the public CLI itself captures live player input.
- **Openness:** **Partial.** Specific inference weights, streaming implementation and Stage-1 training are public. Complete paper-matched corpus and every training/refinement stage were not established in this pass. The paper itself says benchmark images/prompts were internal inputs rather than a separately released dataset at submission; current releases should be audited separately before upgrading that claim.

## 3. Wonder: Video World Model Done Better

- **Year / venue / status:** 2026 arXiv report, [2607.26037](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26037); inspected [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.26037) and [official project](https://wonder-world-model.github.io/).
- **EN:** Generates image- or video-conditioned game-style and general worlds with live camera navigation, sparse full-fidelity memory retrieval, and a camera-regularized few-step causal student.
- **中文：** 从图像或视频生成游戏风格及通用世界，以实时相机导航、稀疏高保真记忆检索和相机正则化少步因果学生支持持续探索。
- **Generates / action conditioning:** The abstract defines users navigating interactively, discovering unseen regions and revisiting prior views. §4 develops pixel-space lattice camera conditioning, sparse context forcing, a timestep-wise mixture of students, and GAN control regularization. The paper explicitly distinguishes the noninteractive bidirectional teacher from the autoregressive few-step student, with a resident streaming runtime (Fig. 3). §4's training description uses two-latent-frame chunks and three students over four denoising steps. A recorded gallery alone is not the evidence of interactivity; the model/interface specification is.
- **Game-domain evidence:** §5.1 constructs **1,000 images explicitly covering gaming scenes**, photographs, AI images, cartoons and art, with five camera trajectories each and VLM-produced navigation keys. The project independently labels **Image-to-Video Game Worlds** and **Video-to-Video Cartoon & Game Worlds**. Thus the game-domain evidence is in the paper's evaluated inputs as well as its official outputs, not an inferred gaming application.
- **Evaluation:** §5.1 compares I2V generation with five streaming baselines (HY-WorldPlay 1.5, RELIC, LingBot-World-Fast, SANA-WM-Streaming, DreamX-World), using visual quality and translation/rotation RPE, plus qualitative revisit memory. §5.2 evaluates 500 dynamic videos with six trajectories each, grouped as retreat/follow/free, against Inspatio-World. Table 1 includes both settings. Reported I2V translation/rotation RPE is 0.0132/0.0784, but these are aggregate benchmark metrics, not game-only scores.
- **Runtime caveat:** The report claims minute-scale **16 FPS** generation with stable latency; the inspected text did not establish a precise inference-GPU count to attach to that headline, so none is asserted here. **32 H200s** describes training, not inference. The website explicitly says displayed videos are linearly interpolated **from 16 to 32 FPS**; displayed playback FPS must not be reported as native model throughput.
- **Official artifacts:** [project](https://wonder-world-model.github.io/), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26037). Inspected project has paper and demo assets, with code/Hugging Face buttons pointing to `#` and marked coming soon. No downloadable official implementation, checkpoints, training data or benchmark package was verified. A comparison paper claiming use of a recommended setting does not prove a public artifact release.
- **Openness:** **Closed** under repository artifact definitions; not a statement that the paper or videos are inaccessible.

## Remaining hold dossiers

### Yume-1.5 is a distinct follow-up, but game evidence is not inherited

[Paper/full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.22096), [current project](https://stdstu12.github.io/YUME-Project/), [shared README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/stdstu12/YUME/main/README.md), [5B weights](https://huggingface.co/stdstu123/Yume-5B-720P).

The full text calls itself **Yume-1.5: A Text-Controlled Interactive World Generation Model**. It adds temporal-spatial-channel modeling (TSCM), linear attention/context compression, bidirectional-to-AR acceleration and text event generation. Table 1 directly compares **Yume** and **Yume1.5**, with IF 0.657 versus 0.836; §5.2.2 tests 30-second generation, partitioned into six five-second intervals. The claimed 12 FPS at 540p uses **one A100**. These are substantive new method/experiment results, so a shared repository and repeated BibTeX key do not make it a duplicate of 2507.17744.

The [HF API](https://huggingface.co/api/models/stdstu123/Yume-5B-720P) verified 15 files, `gated: false`, actual `diffusion_pytorch_model.safetensors` plus Wan2.2 VAE/T5 weights, revision `b117c778405246a0b932d7ec5873b01a4840da3a`. However, the inspected body, figure captions and updated project emphasize realistic worlds, person/camera movement and real-world Yume-Bench. No new explicit game-world experimental case was established in this pass. **Hold this independent paper**, not the earlier Yume record. Revisit the supplementary visual examples for a specifically supported game-world case rather than assuming transfer from the predecessor.

### RELIC has an interactive loop, but UE provenance alone is insufficient

[Paper/full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.04040), [project](https://relic-worldmodel.github.io/).

**RELIC: Interactive Video World Models with Long-Horizon Memory** uses a 14B model and native keyboard inputs represented as scaled multi-hot action vectors. The paper explicitly states streaming user input at inference; its runtime is **16 FPS on four H100s**, not one GPU. Data is 350 photorealistic static UE scenes / about 1,600 minutes, navigated by operators with a collision-constrained camera. The benchmark is **220 Adobe Stock images**, realistic and nonrealistic (cartoons, vector art, oil paintings), comparing action accuracy, selected VBench dimensions and memory against Matrix-Game-2.0 and Hunyuan-GameCraft. This verifies a genuine generator/evaluator and rules out the mistaken “only prerecorded video” objection. It does not establish a specific generated game-domain case under this repository's narrower scope. **Hold**, with the missing item explicitly being game-world output evidence, not combat or real-time interaction. No public code/weights were verified on the inspected project surface.

### minWM is more than framework prose, but game output remains unestablished

[Paper/full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.30263), [repository](https://github.com/shengshu-ai/minWM), [README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shengshu-ai/minWM/main/README.md), [weights](https://huggingface.co/MIN-Lab/minWM).

The paper **minWM: A Full-Stack Open-Source Framework for Real-Time Interactive Video World Models** instantiates Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B and HY1.5-TI2V-8B generators with camera conditioning, AR diffusion, causal ODE/CD and asymmetric DMD. §3.1 uses 480×832, 77-frame clips and four-latent-frame chunks; Fig. 2 explicitly tests **changing camera actions**. §3.2 measures first-frame latency on **one A800, excluding VAE time**; the large speedup compares full-sequence bidirectional latency with first-frame AR latency, not identical end-to-end throughput. §3.3 ablates pose-data quality, control training and batch sizes. Sources include DL3DV reconstructed/re-rendered scenes and OpenVid/other images expanded using WorldPlay.

The [HF API](https://huggingface.co/api/models/MIN-Lab/minWM) verified 29 files (`gated: false`), including HY Action2V/TI2V safetensors and Wan Action2V `model.pt` for bidirectional, AR, ODE/CD and DMD stages; revision `21bd74da43b5a061c0b8ff277515088ccd2c798b`. README's existing-world-model fine-tuning path remains TBD, but functioning from-scratch paths are present. Hence “framework without a demonstrated generator” and “no weights” would both be incorrect descriptions. Nevertheless, the inspected empirical output is generic camera-controllable scenes, not an explicit game-world track; **hold for that output evidence**. Do not substitute WorldPlay's brand or native gaming capability for minWM's own experiment.

## 4. Unbounded: A Generative Infinite Game of Character Life Simulation

- **Year / venue / status:** 2024 preprint, [2410.18975](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.18975); independently inspected [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.18975).
- **EN:** Generates a turn-based life-simulation world from natural-language player input, using a distilled LLM to update character/environment descriptions and hunger, energy, fun and hygiene states, coupled with consistent scene images.
- **中文：** 根据玩家自然语言输入生成回合式生活模拟世界，用蒸馏 LLM 更新角色/环境描述与饥饿、精力、娱乐、卫生状态，并联动生成一致的场景图像。
- **Scope evidence:** §§1/3.3 describe an actual player loop: instructions move the character between environments, direct interactions and update four tracked state dimensions; the resulting text/state conditions image generation. The simulator model, not a separate policy score or standalone character asset, is the evaluated product. Image generation and narrative are integrated into the playable life-sim. This satisfies stepwise player-controlled world generation without requiring continuous video. The paper's aspirational “infinite” or unconstrained language should not become a claim of reliable arbitrary rule invention.
- **Method:** §3.3 distills Gemma-2B from 5,000 synthetic, five-interaction user/simulator examples. A simulator LLM generates state, environment, narrative and image descriptions; a user LLM supplies training interactions. Regional IP-Adapter, personalized character LoRAs and low-step image generation provide consistent visual scenes. The two LLM agents are training-data generators, not the central game-playing evaluation target.
- **Evaluation:** §4.1 includes 5,000 character/environment/prompt triplets for image consistency and prompt alignment, using CLIP, DINO, DreamSim and character presence checks. The separate LLM evaluation uses 100 user-simulator interaction samples with five interaction rounds, GPT-4 pairwise judging, and Table 3 compares the distilled game engine against larger/smaller LLMs and a lower-data ablation. These are model-judge evaluations, not independently verified numeric state-transition correctness or a human gameplay study.
- **Official artifacts:** [project](https://generative-infinite-game.github.io/), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.18975). The primary agent inspected paper/demo links and found no verified downloadable official code, model or data. A “Data coming soon” element is commented-out markup, not evidence of an active release. The paper's AI-tamago link is a related baseline, not an Unbounded implementation.
- **Openness:** **Closed**, limited to verified artifact availability at this audit date.

## 5. Video2Game: accepted PCG playable-content, not another neural-world row

- **Canonical identity:** **Video2Game: Real-time, Interactive, Realistic and Browser-Compatible Environment from a Single Video**, CVPR 2024, pp. 4578–4588. [CVF proceedings](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Xia_Video2Game_Real-time_Interactive_Realistic_and_Browser-Compatible_Environment_from_a_Single_CVPR_2024_paper.html), [arXiv 2404.09833](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.09833), [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.09833). The preprint and CVPR paper are one record.
- **Decision:** **Include as PCG `playable-content`**, specifically reconstructed playable 3D scenes. Do not count it again under interactive video world models or describe it as synthesizing all game code/rules. It was absent from the public indexes when checked in this pass.
- **EN:** Reconstructs video scenes as neural-textured meshes with object-level collision models, then integrates them into browser-playable environments for navigation, driving and object interaction; scene assembly and gameplay configuration remain partly manual.
- **中文：** 将视频场景重建为带神经纹理和对象级碰撞模型的网格，再集成为支持导航、驾驶及物体交互的浏览器可玩环境；场景装配与玩法配置仍有人工环节。
- **Generated playable output evidence:** §3 describes NeRF reconstruction, neural-texture mesh baking and individual physical entities. §3.4 integrates outputs with Sketchbook/Three.js, a GLSL shader and Cannon.js. The gaming application section describes **executable environments with mesh geometry, materials and rigid-body physics encoded in GLB/texture files**, not just screenshots. Demonstrations include Gardenvase navigation and shooting footballs to knock the vase off its table, plus KITTI-360 running/coin collection, driving and collisions with reconstructed roadside cars. The project has an actual [Gardenvase game entry](https://video2game.github.io/src/garden/index.html) instantiating `new Video2Game.World('build/assets/world.glb')`; merely watching a robot simulation video is not the basis for inclusion.
- **Automation boundary — explicit primary evidence:** §3.3 allows **mass and friction to be set manually or estimated by an LLM**; collision shape is chosen according to object/task. Appendix C states that overlapping boundaries between the 14 KITTI blocks are **manually defined** before mesh assembly. Fracture animation is precomputed. The author [readme.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/video2game/video2game/main/readme.md) tells users to **manually configure** screen size, intrinsics, rendering meshes and collision-model paths in `game_dev/src/ts/world/World.ts`; it warns that advanced Cannon.js physical interactions require laborious tasks and recommends Unreal for easier development. Thus this is a reconstruction-to-playable-content pipeline with authored runtime integration, not automatic invention of collecting/shooting/driving mechanics. Its central generator still materially creates the playable environment integrated by the paper, satisfying the scope's playable-content rule.
- **Evaluation:** §4 benchmarks Gardenvase, KITTI-360 and an indoor VR-NeRF scene. Table 1 evaluates novel-view quality and interactive compatibility; geometry/mesh comparisons and component ablations test the reconstruction and baking method. Table 4 measures runtime across hardware/platforms. The reported **over-100 FPS** demonstrations are conventional engine **rendering/simulation rates after offline scene construction**, not neural video generation throughput, and must not be mixed with GameNGen/SANA/Wonder FPS. Game interaction examples are demonstrations, not a standardized gameplay success benchmark. The robot track is separate and not the admission basis.
- **Official artifacts and actual files:** [repository](https://github.com/video2game/video2game), [exact lowercase readme.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/video2game/video2game/main/readme.md), [project/game](https://video2game.github.io/), [game source](https://github.com/video2game/video2game/tree/main/game_dev). A recursive official tree response (`truncated: false`) verified `train.py`, `extract_mesh.py`, `baking_pretrain.py`, `baking_pretrain_export.py`, `collision_model_generate.py`, `eval_nerf.py`, `eval_textured_mesh.py`, game TypeScript sources, built JavaScript, GLB worlds/actors and collider classes. Do not misclassify the repo as absent because uppercase `README.md` returns 404.
- **Openness:** **Partial.** The live README marks NeRF, mesh extraction/postprocessing, baking pretraining, collision generation and evaluation released, but **Baking Finetune Module** and **GPT-4 Query** remain unchecked. Public `game_dev` provides an executable engine scaffold with documented manual setup, not a verified one-click reproduction of every paper scene and physics interaction. Full pipeline, asset loading and gameplay were not executed in this audit. The project warns about cross-origin asset-loading issues; no browser-security setting was changed during inspection.

## Verification limitations

Primary arXiv HTML, first-party project pages, exact raw READMEs and model-file manifests were used. Shared/revised pages were distinguished from historical paper identities. Model binaries, live user-control demos and full training/evaluation pipelines were not executed. No performance claim here is independently reproduced. Inclusion recommendations rest on the paper's described generation/control/evaluation, not on whether the current public artifact can reproduce every result.
